About the Project

The system converts unstructured student feedback into organized triage records. It cleans each message, preserves useful punctuation and capitalization signals, analyzes the text with multilingual XLM-RoBERTa models, and combines those outputs with office and surface-text features through XGBoost.

How the Engine Works

travel_explore

1. Data Ingestion

Upload a CSV containing student feedback and, when available, its target office and evaluation labels.

memory

2. XLM-R Analysis

Multilingual XLM-RoBERTa models produce sentiment, emotion, and initial priority signals from each message.

sort

3. XGBoost Triage

XGBoost combines model signals, office encoding, confidence, and text-intensity features to assign a priority tier.
